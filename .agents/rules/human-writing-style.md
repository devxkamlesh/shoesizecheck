# Human Writing Style

All written content (website copy, SEO text, meta descriptions, FAQs, blog posts, page descriptions) must read like it was written by a person, not generated by AI.

## Banned Words and Phrases

Never use these words or phrases in any content:

- ultimate, comprehensive, cutting-edge, game-changer, game-changing
- revolutionize, revolutionary, transformative, innovative, groundbreaking
- delve, dive into, embark, tapestry, landscape, paradigm
- harness, leverage (as verb), utilize (use "use" instead)
- elevate, empower, foster, bolster, spearhead
- multifaceted, myriad, plethora, robust, seamless, streamline
- cornerstone, linchpin, bedrock, underpinning, underscores
- pivotal, crucial (overused), essential (overused when paired with superlatives)
- navigate (metaphorical), realm, arena, sphere
- in today's [X], in the world of, it's worth noting, it is important to note
- not just X but also Y (as a recurring pattern)
- whether you're a [X] or a [Y]
- designed to [verb], crafted to [verb]
- look no further, without further ado, buckle up
- at the end of the day, when it comes to
- as we'll explore, as we delve deeper
- a testament to, stands as a beacon
- helps ensure, plays a crucial role, serves as a
- deeply, highly (as empty intensifiers)
- resonate, showcase (as verbs)

## Writing Rules

1. **Use simple, direct language.** Write like you're explaining something to a friend. Say "use" not "utilize." Say "help" not "empower." Say "important" not "pivotal."

2. **Vary sentence length.** Mix short sentences with longer ones. Don't write paragraph after paragraph of medium-length, evenly structured sentences. That's a dead giveaway.

3. **Be specific, not vague.** Instead of "this tool offers a comprehensive solution," write "this tool converts US 9 to EU 42 in one click." Real examples beat abstract claims.

4. **Drop the sales pitch tone.** Don't oversell. State what the tool does plainly. Let the reader decide if it's useful. Avoid stacking adjectives like "fast, accurate, and reliable."

5. **Use contractions.** Write "it's" not "it is." Write "don't" not "do not." Write "you'll" not "you will." Normal people use contractions.

6. **Start sentences differently.** Don't begin every paragraph with the same structure. Avoid the pattern: "[Topic] is [description]. It [does thing]. This [result]." Mix it up.

7. **Skip the throat-clearing.** Don't start sections with "When it comes to..." or "In today's world..." or "It's no secret that..." Just get to the point.

8. **Avoid triple lists in every sentence.** AI loves writing "X, Y, and Z" in every single sentence. Use pairs sometimes. Use single items. Use four items occasionally. Break the pattern.

9. **Don't summarize what you just said.** If you explained how something works, don't end with "In summary, this tool provides..." The reader just read it.

10. **Use "is" and "are" normally.** AI avoids simple copula sentences. It's fine to write "The EU system is gender-neutral" instead of "The EU system, recognized as gender-neutral, offers..."

11. **No em-dash overuse.** One or two em dashes per article is fine. Five or more is a red flag.

12. **Write asymmetric paragraphs.** Some paragraphs should be 2 sentences. Others can be 5. Don't make every paragraph exactly 3-4 sentences long.

13. **Include opinions and recommendations when appropriate.** Say "we recommend measuring in the evening" not "it is generally recommended to measure in the evening." Take a stance.
